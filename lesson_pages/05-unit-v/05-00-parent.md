# Unit V: Advanced gem5 Topics

gem5 supports several other features that may be useful for your simulations; these are covered here.

> By the end of this unit, you will be able to:
> 
> - **Incorporate** acceleration and sampling methods into simulations as appropriate.
> - **Modify** and **use** customized disk images to meet a simulation goal.
> - **Use** multisim to run multiple simulations in parallel.
> - **Incorporate** power models into gem5 simulations.
